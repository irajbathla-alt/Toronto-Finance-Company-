Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Important: By applying for financing through Toronto Finance Company, you authorize us to collect, use, verify and disclose your personal and business information to one or more prospective lenders, funders, financial institutions, credit reporting agencies and service providers for the purpose of arranging, evaluating and administering business financing.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Toronto Finance Company (“TFC,” “we,” “us” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, stores and protects personal and business information when you use our website, application portal, client dashboard or related financing advisory services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact information, including names, business names, addresses, telephone numbers and email addresses.
  • Identity information, including dates of birth, identification documents and ownership information.
  • Business information, including legal name, industry, registration details, employees, revenue, banking relationships and financing needs.
  • Financial information, including bank statements, tax documents, financial statements, payment history, account information and existing financing obligations.
  • Credit-related information, including personal and business credit reports, scores and information obtained from credit bureaus or other lawful sources.
  • Technical and security information, including account identifiers, device information, browser information, IP address, timestamps and application activity.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account and financing application.
  • To assess your financing request and organize your supporting documents.
  • To verify identity, business ownership and information supplied in an application.
  • To identify financing options and communicate with prospective lenders or funders.
  • To conduct credit, fraud-prevention, anti-money-laundering and due-diligence checks.
  • To communicate application status, document requirements, offers and lender decisions.
  • To maintain audit trails, security records and regulatory or legal records.
  • To improve our services, systems, underwriting tools and client experience.

4. Authority to Submit to Multiple Lenders

By submitting an application or otherwise instructing us to seek financing, you grant Toronto Finance Company full authority to package, present, submit, resubmit and discuss your application and supporting information with multiple lenders, funders, financial institutions, brokers, referral partners and financing platforms that we reasonably believe may offer a suitable financing solution.

This authority includes permission to disclose your application, bank statements, financial records, identification, corporate records, credit information and other supporting documents to those parties. You understand that each lender may apply its own underwriting criteria, request additional documents, conduct its own verification and credit review, and independently approve, decline or modify any financing proposal.

5. Credit and Background Checks

You authorize us and our prospective lending partners, agents and service providers to obtain personal and business credit reports and to make lawful inquiries necessary to evaluate, process, renew, monitor or administer a financing application. Where required, additional lender-specific consent may also be requested.

6. Sharing of Information

We may share information with prospective lenders and funders, credit bureaus, identity-verification providers, document-storage providers, electronic-signature providers, payment or banking technology providers, legal and professional advisors, government or regulatory authorities, and other service providers that assist us in delivering our services. We do not sell sensitive personal information for unrelated commercial purposes.

7. Consent and Withdrawal

You may withdraw consent for future use or disclosure by contacting us. Withdrawal will not affect disclosures already made, lender submissions already initiated, records we are legally required to retain, or actions already taken in reliance on your authorization. Withdrawal may prevent us from continuing to arrange financing.

8. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain application and audit records, respond to disputes, comply with lender, legal and regulatory requirements, prevent fraud and enforce agreements.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure, and clients should use secure passwords and avoid sending sensitive documents through unsecured channels.

10. Electronic Records and Audit Trail

We may record application submissions, field changes, document uploads, account activity, consent events, timestamps, device details and electronic-signature events. These records may be used to demonstrate what information was provided, when it was provided and what authorizations were granted.

11. Access and Correction

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions and identity verification. Certain records may not be deleted where retention is required for legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or contractual purposes.

12. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar tools for security, website functionality, analytics and service improvement. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies.

13. Third-Party Services

Information submitted directly to a lender, electronic-signature provider, banking connector or other third party may also be governed by that party’s privacy policy and terms.

14. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be posted on this website with its effective date.

15. Contact

Questions, access requests or privacy concerns may be submitted through the contact information published on the Toronto Finance Company website.

This policy is intended as a practical business privacy document and should be reviewed by qualified Canadian privacy counsel before production use.
