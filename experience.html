What We Can Help You Achieve

The right capital can create breathing room and opportunity.

Your business may need predictable financing, on-demand access to capital or a repayment structure that moves with cash flow. We focus on three core solutions.

Business

Term Loan

$10K to $500K

Use predictable financing for expansion, inventory, equipment, improvements or other planned business needs, with fixed repayment over a set term.

Business

Line of Credit

$10K to $500K

Create a financial cushion for timing gaps, unexpected expenses or opportunities, while paying only for the capital you actually use.

Flex

Funds Advance

$5K to $500K

Access working capital with flexible repayment designed to better align with the natural ebbs and flows of your business.

Your Outcome Matters

We start with what you are trying to accomplish.

Funding is only useful when it improves the position of the business. We look at why the capital is needed, when it is needed, how repayment will affect cash flow and which structure gives the business the clearest path forward. Sometimes that means pursuing financing. Sometimes it means recommending a different amount, structure or timing.
